What a journey it's been in the Hoenn region! After weeks of exploration, training, and countless Pokémon encounters, I've finally conquered the Pokémon League in Ruby. The Elite Four and Champion Steven proved to be formidable opponents, but my team pulled through! Let me share the highlights of this incredible adventure and some hard-earned tips.

**The Zigzagoon Strategy**

Right from the start, I implemented a game-changing strategy: catching 5 Zigzagoons early in the game! Here's why this is such a powerful tip for Ruby and Sapphire players – Zigzagoons have the Pickup ability, and in these games, they can find Rare Candies even at low levels! (In later generations, they need to be at higher levels to find the really good stuff, but Ruby/Sapphire Zigzagoons are incredible right from the beginning.)

Having those 5 Zigzagoons in my party as I explored meant I was constantly finding useful items, especially those precious Rare Candies that made leveling so much easier throughout my journey.

**The Road to Victory**

My final team for the Elite Four was perhaps not the most balanced: Swampert (my trusty Mudkip starter), Gardevoir, Swellow, Hariyama, and Sharpedo – all caught in classic Poké Balls, of course! The battles were intense:

*   **Sidney (Dark-type):** Hariyama's Fighting-type moves dominated this battle, though his Absol gave me some trouble.
*   **Phoebe (Ghost-type):** Those Dusclops were tanky! Swellow's speed and Sharpedo's bite attacks helped push through.
*   **Glacia (Ice-type):** This was where Gardevoir truly shined! After setting up with 6 Calm Minds (got pretty lucky), she absolutely *crushed* Glacia's entire team. It was beautiful to watch!
*   **Drake (Dragon-type):** The toughest of the Elite Four! My team was probably average level at best, but we managed to pull through with smart switches and type advantages.
*   **Champion Steven:** What an incredible battle! His Metagross was a powerhouse, but my diverse team managed to overcome his balanced roster.

**Living Pokédex Progress in Hoenn**

The real challenge wasn't just beating the League – it was catching them all! Working my way toward each gym, I made sure to catch every different species I encountered, building up my collection steadily. Here's where I stood with my Ruby Living Pokédex by the end:

*   **Gym Challenge Strategy:** Caught as many different species as possible on each route leading to the next gym
*   **Zigzagoon MVP Status:** Those 5 Zigzagoons with Pickup were absolute game-changers throughout the entire journey
*   **Team Highlights:** Sharpedo was the absolute MVP against Liza and Tate (Gym 7) – those Water/Dark-type moves were perfect for their Psychic-types!
*   **Still to Catch:** Haven't tackled the legendary challenges yet – Feebas, the Regis, and Rayquaza are still on my to-do list!

**Pro Tips for Ruby Trainers**

Here are some strategies that really helped me during my Hoenn adventure:

1. **The Zigzagoon Pickup Squad:** This is the big one! Catch 5 Zigzagoons early in the game and keep them in your party. In Ruby and Sapphire, they can find Rare Candies even at low levels (unlike later generations where they need to be higher level for the good stuff). This single strategy will transform your entire playthrough!

2. **Gym-by-Gym Collection:** As you work toward each gym, catch every different species you encounter on the routes. This steady approach builds your Living Pokédex naturally while you progress.

3. **Sharpedo for Liza and Tate:** If you're struggling with the Psychic-type twins at Mossdeep Gym, Sharpedo is your answer! Those Dark-type moves are super effective, and it can handle both of their Pokémon.

4. **Gardevoir + Calm Mind = Victory:** Don't underestimate setup moves! My Gardevoir with 6 Calm Minds absolutely demolished Glacia's Ice-types in the Elite Four.

5. **Average Levels Can Win:** You don't need to be massively overleveled for the Elite Four. Smart strategy and type advantages can carry you through with a reasonably leveled team.

**What's Next?**

With Ruby conquered and the Elite Four defeated, I made the decision to switch over to Pokémon Emerald – but not just for the expanded storyline! I'm planning to use some ACE (Arbitrary Code Execution) techniques in Emerald to unlock Mystery Gift events, giving me access to Birth Island, Navel Rock, and most importantly, unlimited Rare Candies after beating the Elite Four.

My strategy is to use those Rare Candies to power up my Ruby team, and I've also caught a Shroomish that I plan to level up and teach both Spore and False Swipe – the perfect combination for safely catching all those legendary Pokémon I still need in Ruby! Having a dedicated catcher with sleep-inducing moves and a move that leaves Pokémon at 1 HP will make tackling Feebas, the Regis, and Rayquaza much more manageable.

The beauty of this approach is that I can experience Emerald's enhanced content while also setting myself up for success back in Ruby. It's like having the best of both worlds!

My Paldea adventure in Scarlet also continues alongside this, and the variety between regions keeps this challenge fresh and exciting.

**Reflecting on the Ruby Experience**

Ruby was such a solid introduction to the Hoenn region. The Zigzagoon strategy alone made this one of my most resource-rich playthroughs ever, and building my team around the Pokémon I actually encountered (rather than planning ahead) led to some surprisingly effective combinations.

Who would have thought that Sharpedo would be such a gym-crushing powerhouse, or that Gardevoir's setup potential would be so devastating against the Elite Four?
