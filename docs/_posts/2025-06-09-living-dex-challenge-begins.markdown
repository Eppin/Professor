Welcome to the start of an epic adventure! I've always loved the world of Pokémon, and I'm now embarking on a personal quest I've dubbed the "Ultimate Living Pokédex Challenge": to create a Living Pokédex for *every main Pokémon game*. That means catching, and keeping, one of every single Pokémon species, all meticulously organized. It's a marathon, not a sprint, but I'm excited to document every step!

**My Guiding Principles for this Challenge:**

*   **The Classic Catch:** Whenever possible, every Pokémon will be caught in a standard, red-and-white Poké Ball. There's just something classic about it!
*   **Trainer ID Consistency:** I'll aim to have as few different Original Trainer Numbers (IDNo.) and game origins as possible across the entire collection. This means trying to complete as much of a Pokédex within a single game file before moving on or relying on other saves.
*   **Self-Reliance:** The primary goal is to catch them all myself. Breeding will only be used as a last resort if a Pokémon is unobtainable otherwise in a specific game or version. Trading will only occur between my own games, using Pokémon that I am the Original Trainer (OT) of, to maintain the integrity of the collection.

**Kicking Things Off in Paldea!**

The journey starts with Pokémon Scarlet! I was lucky enough to get my hands on the new Nintendo Switch 2 last week. After the initial setup and installing the latest update for Scarlet and Violet (which thankfully included some much-needed framerate improvements!), I dove straight into the vibrant Paldea region. The new console feels great, and Paldea looks fantastic on it!

My current focus is simple: catch everything I encounter, sticking to my Poké Ball rule. So far, I've managed to defeat the first two Gym Leaders – Katy (the Bug-type Gym Leader) and then Brassius (the Grass-type Gym Leader). Next up, I'm heading towards Levincia to challenge Iono, the Electric-type Gym Leader.

**Early Game Tip: Raid Power!**

For anyone starting out in Paldea or looking for a good way to get experience and items, here's a tip: hit those Tera Raids! I've been trying to do as many as possible. Start with the 1-star raids, and as your team gets stronger, move up to 2-star raids. Once you've earned three Gym Badges, you'll unlock 3-star raids, which are a great source for better XP Candies to level up your team faster.

Stay tuned for more updates as I progress through Paldea and beyond!
