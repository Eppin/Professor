My Emerald adventure is taking a completely different approach compared to my methodical Ruby playthrough! Instead of catching everything along the way, I'm doing a focused gym rush with one very specific goal in mind: reaching the post-game so I can implement ACE (Arbitrary Code Execution) techniques. This strategic approach will ultimately benefit my entire Living Pokédex challenge across multiple games.

**Current Progress: The Strategic Sprint**

I'm currently at Fortree City, having just reached Gym 7! This has been a fascinating exercise in restraint – normally I'd be catching every new species I encounter, but this time I'm deliberately holding back until I can unlock the ACE capabilities that will make my collecting efforts much more efficient.

*   **Current Location:** Fortree City (Gym 7)
*   **Badges Earned:** 6 out of 8 Gym Badges
*   **Catching Status:** Deliberately avoiding catches until post-ACE setup
*   **Team Strategy:** Using traded Pokémon for efficient progression

**The Trading Strategy**

My approach to building a team has been quite unique this time around:

*   **Started with Treecko:** Chose my Grass starter, then immediately traded it to my Ruby save for some quick leveling
*   **Cross-Game Team Building:** Traded the leveled Treecko back, plus brought over a Horsea and Combusken from my Sapphire save
*   **Mixed Origins:** Having Pokémon from different saves gives me a solid, diverse team without needing to catch and train from scratch

This trading approach has let me focus purely on progression rather than team building, which is perfect for my current ACE-focused goals.

**Why the ACE Rush?**

For those unfamiliar, ACE (Arbitrary Code Execution) will allow me to:

*   **Unlock Mystery Gift Events:** Access to Birth Island, Navel Rock, and other event locations
*   **Generate Unlimited Rare Candies:** Perfect for powering up my teams across all games
*   **Streamline Future Catching:** Having proper tools and levels will make completing my Ruby Living Pokédex much more manageable

The plan is to use Emerald's ACE capabilities to enhance my entire Hoenn experience, then return to Ruby with unlimited Rare Candies and the ability to train that perfect Breloom with Spore + False Swipe for legendary catching.

**Different Goals for Different Games**

It's worth noting that my approach to these two Hoenn games has different end goals:

*   **Ruby:** Regional Living Pokédex (focusing on the Hoenn Pokédex species)
*   **Emerald:** National Living Pokédex (aiming for all Pokémon available across generations)

This is why the ACE setup in Emerald is so crucial – having access to event Pokémon and unlimited resources will make completing a full National Pokédex much more feasible, while Ruby will serve as my definitive regional Hoenn collection.

**The Sapphire Connection**

Of course, for both Ruby and Emerald Living Pokédexes, I'll be relying on my Sapphire save for version exclusives. Having all three Hoenn games means I can obtain every possible species without needing to trade with other players - staying true to my self-reliance principle while still achieving completionist goals across the region.

**Gym Progress Highlights**

Even without catching wild Pokémon, the gym battles have been engaging:

*   **Different Gym Leaders:** Seeing Juan instead of Wallace at Sootopolis will be interesting when I get there
*   **Double Battle Focus:** Emerald's emphasis on doubles is already apparent, even during the gym rush
*   **Team Aqua vs. Team Magma:** The expanded storyline with both teams is compelling, even when I'm trying to rush through

**The Restraint Challenge**

Not catching Pokémon as I encounter them has been surprisingly difficult! My natural instinct is to catch everything new I see, but I keep reminding myself that the ACE payoff will be worth it. Plus, once I have unlimited Rare Candies and proper catching tools, I'll be able to approach the Living Pokédex much more systematically.

**What's Next?**

My immediate goals are straightforward:

1. **Reach Gym 8:** Push through to Sootopolis and face Juan
2. **Conquer Elite Four:** Get to the post-game content as quickly as possible
3. **Implement ACE:** Set up Mystery Gift access and unlimited Rare Candies
4. **Return to Ruby:** Use the enhanced tools to complete my Ruby Living Pokédex

**Looking Forward**

This focused approach to Emerald is teaching me a lot about different ways to experience the same region. Where Ruby was about methodical collection and team building, Emerald is about strategic progression and setting up for long-term success across multiple games.

Once I've got the ACE setup complete, I'll be able to approach both games with a completely different toolkit – proper levels, perfect catching Pokémon, and access to event locations that would otherwise be impossible to reach.

The anticipation is building as I get closer to unlocking these game-changing capabilities!
