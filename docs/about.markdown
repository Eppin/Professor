Welcome to my Living Pokédex Journey!

This blog chronicles my adventure to complete a Living Pokédex for every main Pokémon game. Join me as I share my progress, the challenges I encounter, helpful tips I discover, and the milestones I reach along the way.

Whether you're a fellow Pokédex completer, a Pokémon enthusiast, or just curious about the dedication it takes, I hope you find something interesting here!

Feel free to follow along and share your own Pokémon adventures!
